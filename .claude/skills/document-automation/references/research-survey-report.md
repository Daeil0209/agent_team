# document-automation: Research And Survey Reports
Use when the document is a research report, survey report, literature review, patent landscape, or source-based decision report.

## Research/Survey Reports
- Freeze supported question, primary reader, and decision before intake or drafting.
- Freeze a method-shaped skeleton: question scope, source-family coverage, inclusion/exclusion logic, cross-check state, uncertainty labels, and update-trace surfaces.
- Structure outputs in reader decision order.
- Decision summary comes first.
- Judgment-driving analysis follows the decision summary.
- Source-data, examples, and application method follow when reuse or decision value depends on them.
- Evidence detail and update-ready trace follow the reader-decision layers.
- Consume the `researcher` evidence packet for source-family method, search strategy, patent filters, claim-evidence tiers, and update trace.
- This reference owns reader structure and generation quality.
- `researcher` owns evidence acquisition.
- Declare the selected methodology framework before prose fill when it shapes reader trust.
- Methodology examples include PRISMA for literature, Cochrane for systematic review, WIPO for patent landscape, or equivalent.
- Show cited-source roles from the evidence packet when material.
- Role imbalance becomes a document-character defect after the evidence owner supplies or blocks the basis.
- Required visual palette evaluation covers technology taxonomy diagram, comparison matrix, distribution map, competitive positioning map, and claim-evidence mapping table.
- Body sections advance the reader's decision.
- Appendices hold supporting detail, raw data, and extended methodology.
- Body sections that read as appendix material are structure defects.

## Resolve Next Owner And Action
- Return supported question, reader decision, method skeleton, evidence-packet dependency, visual-palette decision, and appendix/body boundary to the active document-automation workflow.
- If evidence method or source basis is missing, route to `researcher` before generation claims source-backed quality.
